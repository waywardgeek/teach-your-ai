# Carryover chapter

Read this with [book-execution-workflow.md](book-execution-workflow.md) before executing a chapter. For an existing book, use its actual carryover chapter.

This is a starter template, **not a record of a completed run**. Fill it with observed lessons and accepted decisions. The workflow explains how to work; carryover preserves what earlier builds established and what the next iteration must not forget.

**Before every whole-book execution, fold the recorded lessons into the relevant chapters.** Record where they went. Carryover is not a second hidden specification or a substitute for fixing the book. Never supply target solutions or private grading details here.

## Accepted starting state

Which chapters and implementation has the human accepted? Where are the solution, public execution instructions, and grader entry points?

## Fixes and lessons for the next iteration

For each relevant issue, record concisely:

- What failed or was worse than intended.
- Why it happened, distinguishing an established cause from a hypothesis.
- What changed in the implementation, grader, or explanation.
- What was actually checked and what remains unverified.
- Where the book now teaches the correction, or which chapter still needs repair before the next run.

Link to existing review notes and corrected chapters rather than duplicating long reports. Preserve the reason for non-obvious decisions. Do not present an unresolved proposal as an accepted requirement.

## Cross-cutting execution lessons

Keep established lessons that apply across chapters, with links to their owning documents. Remove stale advice when those documents change.

## Environment and open questions

Record needed setup, unavailable checks, known limitations, and decisions still requiring the human. Never include secret values.
